Pay-when-happy automation agreement template

A pay-when-happy automation agreement is a fixed-price agreement where you pay for an automation only once it's running in your business and you're happy with it. At Mike Automations that means one workflow for a fixed $2,000 ($1,500 for the first 3 firms, in return for honest feedback), no deposit and no hourly billing. The acceptance test is the heart of it: before any work starts, you write down what "working" means as checks you can tick, so there's no argument later about what "done" means.

Why the acceptance test matters

"Happy" on its own is hard to check. A short list of success criteria turns it into something you can test on real work in your business. If the workflow meets them and you're happy with it, you pay. If not, you owe nothing for that build, and Michael removes anything you don't want to keep. Write each line as something you could see with your own eyes, in your own inbox or folders.

What "working" can mean

Examples, for illustration

Good success criteria are specific and easy to check. Some examples for a document-chasing workflow:

  • Every new client gets a checklist and reminders until their documents are in.
  • Every client with items outstanding gets a reminder every 7 days until they're in.
  • Every upload is filed in the right client folder and checked off.
  • A morning list of who's still missing what reaches me each working day.
  • Nothing goes to a client before I've approved the wording.
  • I can pause it myself, using the one-page guide.

Not sure what your workflow should be? Build your plan first, then turn it into an agreement here.

How to use this template

  1. Fill in your business, the workflow, your tools and what "working" means.
  2. Print it or save it as a PDF, or send the draft to Michael.
  3. Michael replies by email. The draft isn't binding until he confirms it in writing, and work starts only after you accept it by email.
