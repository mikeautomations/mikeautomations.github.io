Edit the words in [brackets] before you send. A person should read every message before it goes out.

Booking confirmation (edit the words in brackets)
----------------------------------------
Hi [first name], thanks for booking [property name] from [arrival date] to [departure date]. You're all set. Check-in is from [time] and checkout is by [time]. I'll send the address details and check-in instructions on the morning you arrive. If you'd like to arrive earlier or leave later, or have any questions before then, reply here. [your name]

----------------------------------------
Day before checkout (edit the words in brackets)
----------------------------------------
Hi [first name], a quick note for tomorrow. Checkout is by [time]. Before you go: [leave the key in the key safe / lock the door and post the key through the letterbox], put rubbish in the [bin location], and load any dishes into the dishwasher. No need to strip the beds. If you need a later checkout, reply here and I'll tell you if it's possible. Thanks for staying with us. [your name]

Review request, the day after checkout
----------------------------------------
Hi [first name], thanks for staying at [property name]. We hope the trip went well. If you have a minute, a review would help other guests know what to expect. And if anything wasn't right, please tell us here so we can fix it. [your name]
