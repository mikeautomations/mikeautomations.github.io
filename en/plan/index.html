Build your automation plan

The plan builder turns the job that eats your week into a written automation plan: the steps that run on their own, the steps you approve, which of your tools it uses, what stays with you and the fixed price. It covers 47 workflows for small service businesses, each taken from a guide on this site. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to book a call.

How it works

  1. Pick your business. Accountants, trades, letting agents, solicitors, clinics, agencies and more.
  2. Pick the job, or describe it in one sentence. The builder shows the closest matching workflows.
  3. Tick the tools you use (optional). You get the plan: each step, who does it, the price and how to start.

The interactive builder needs JavaScript. Every workflow is listed below by business type, with a link to the guide that explains it step by step.

Every workflow, by business type

Accountants & bookkeepers

  • Chasing Self Assessment records: Each client gets their own list of missing records and reminders until it's complete, before the 31 January deadline.
  • Getting bank statements and receipts every month: Requests each client's statements, receipts and invoices on a fixed day, collects them in one place and chases what's missing.
  • VAT quarter records chasing: Asks each VAT client for the quarter's records as the period ends and reminds them until the file is complete.
  • Year-end records from limited companies: Starts each director's records request on their company's own year end, chases it and warns you early about tight files.
  • Tax season document chasing (US): Each client gets a checklist of missing W-2s, 1099s and receipts, an upload link and reminders until it's complete.
  • Collecting W-9s before 1099 season: Requests a W-9 from every payee before the first payment and follows up until it's in.
  • Engagement letters out and signed: Fills your approved letter from the intake form, queues it for a person to check, sends it for e-signature and chases it.
  • Collecting payroll changes from clients: Sends every payroll client a short form before each cut-off and chases anyone who hasn't answered, even with "no changes".
  • Client ID and AML checks: Requests ID and due diligence documents from new clients, chases what's missing and puts the file in front of your compliance person.

Trades & contractors

  • Following up estimates and quotes: Every open estimate gets polite follow-ups on a fixed schedule until the customer says yes or no.
  • WhatsApp quote follow-up: Three short WhatsApp messages after each quote, then it stops, with every reply flagged to you.
  • Texting back missed calls: Anyone whose call you miss gets a text within a minute, a few questions about the job and a way to book.
  • Tracking subcontractor insurance certificates: Collects a current COI from every sub, logs expiry dates and asks for the renewal before it lapses.
  • Change orders signed before the work: The crew logs a change on a phone, the office prices it and it goes for e-signature before any extra work starts.
  • Maintenance agreement renewals: Keeps one list of every plan, reminds customers before it lapses and asks them to book their covered tune-ups.
  • EICR remedial work follow-up: Sends the remedial quote within a day of an unsatisfactory EICR and follows up on dates so the work is approved in time.
  • Out-of-hours emergency call triage: Every out-of-hours caller answers the same short questions, so only real emergencies wake the engineer on call.
  • Job sign-off and the final invoice: Completion photos and the customer's signature on a phone, then a draft final invoice the same day.

Letting agents & property managers

  • Tenant repair requests: Every repair report comes in on one form with photos, urgent jobs go to a person and the tenant is updated at each step.
  • Getting landlords to approve repairs: Checks each job against the landlord's limit, sends the quote for approval, chases on a schedule and logs the decision.
  • Gas safety, EICR and EPC reminders: One list of every property and certificate, with renewals started weeks before each expiry date.
  • Right to rent follow-up checks: Tracks every time-limited right to rent and asks the tenant for evidence well before the follow-up date.
  • Deposit protection 30-day clock: Starts a 30-day clock when a deposit arrives and chases protection and the prescribed information until a person confirms both.
  • Rent review and lease renewal reminders: Tracks each tenancy's review date, asks the landlord for a decision and drafts the notice for a person to check.
  • Property inspection scheduling: Books each inspection, gives the tenant written notice, reminds everyone and sends the report to the landlord.

Solicitors & conveyancers

  • New client intake for solicitors: An instant reply with a short form, a conflict check before any booking, the client care letter and ID requests.
  • Conveyancing client updates: A short message to the client each time their file reaches a milestone, so fewer "any update?" calls.
  • Source of funds document chasing: Sends the right document list for each way of funding, chases what's missing and shows which files are ready for review.
  • Will questionnaire before the meeting: Sends the questionnaire the day a client gets in touch, reminds until it's complete, then offers the meeting.

Mortgage & insurance brokers

  • Mortgage document collection: Sends each client the checklist for their situation, chases only what's missing and shows whose pack is ready.
  • Insurance renewal reminders: Every renewal date triggers a details request, reminders and a renewal email prepared for a broker to check.

Clinics (physio, dental)

  • Patient intake forms before the first visit: Sends the right intake form when a new patient books and reminds until it's back.
  • Rebooking patients mid-treatment: Finds patients with sessions left and no next booking, offers a booking link and hands the rest to a person.
  • Dental recall follow-up: Follows up patients who ignore the first recall reminder and gives the front desk one call list.
  • Appointment reminders and no-shows: Confirms every booking, reminds the day before with an easy way to confirm or move, and lists who didn't reply.

Salons, gyms & studios

  • Cancellations, deposits and empty slots: Shows your policy at booking, fills cancelled slots from a waiting list and keeps a log of deposits kept or refunded.
  • Failed membership payments: Picks up each failed payment, sends a friendly link to update details and puts members still unpaid on a call list.

Cleaning & garden services

  • Recurring cleaning admin: Builds the week's rota, reminds clients, sends each cleaner a job sheet and invoices after each visit.
  • Seasonal renewals: Asks last season's customers to sign up again with a price and link, reminds the undecided and books the first round.

Marketing agencies

  • New client onboarding: From an accepted proposal to a ready kickoff: contract, first invoice, brief form, files and access requests.
  • Monthly client reports: Pulls the month's figures into each client's report on a fixed day, so a person only writes the commentary.

Recruitment agencies

  • Weekly timesheet chasing: Reminds workers when the week ends, asks the client's approver once hours arrive and lists gaps on payroll morning.
  • Candidate compliance documents: One checklist per role, an automatic request when a candidate registers, reminders and an expiry list.

Another service business

  • Invoice payment reminders: Polite reminders before and after the due date that stop the moment a client pays.
  • Replying to new enquiries fast: Every enquiry lands in one list and gets an honest first reply straight away, with the questions you need answered.
  • Asking every customer for a review: One short request with your review link after each job, and one reminder.
  • AI that sorts enquiries and drafts replies: An AI agent sorts incoming messages and drafts replies; a person approves before anything reaches a customer.
