Build your automation plan

The plan builder turns the job that eats your week into a written automation plan: the steps that run on their own, the steps you approve, which of your tools it uses, what stays with you and the fixed price. It covers 108 workflows for small service businesses, each taken from a guide on this site. It runs in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to book a call.

How it works

  1. Pick your business. Accountants, trades, letting agents, solicitors, clinics, agencies and more.
  2. Pick the job, or describe it in one sentence. The builder shows the closest matching workflows.
  3. Tick the tools you use (optional). You get the plan: each step, who does it, the price and how to start.

The interactive builder needs JavaScript. Every workflow is listed below by business type, with a link to the guide that explains it step by step.

Every workflow, by business type

Accountants & bookkeepers

  • Chasing Self Assessment records: Each client gets their own list of missing records and reminders until it's complete, before the 31 January deadline.
  • Getting bank statements and receipts every month: Requests each client's statements, receipts and invoices on a fixed day, collects them in one place and chases what's missing.
  • VAT quarter records chasing: Asks each VAT client for the quarter's records as the period ends and reminds them until the file is complete.
  • Year-end records from limited companies: Starts each director's records request on their company's own year end, chases it and warns you early about tight files.
  • Tax season document chasing (US): Each client gets a checklist of missing W-2s, 1099s and receipts, an upload link and reminders until it's complete.
  • Collecting W-9s before 1099 season: Requests a W-9 from every payee before the first payment and follows up until it's in.
  • Engagement letters out and signed: Fills your approved letter from the intake form, queues it for a person to check, sends it for e-signature and chases it.
  • Collecting payroll changes from clients: Sends every payroll client a short form before each cut-off and chases anyone who hasn't answered, even with "no changes".
  • Client ID and AML checks: Requests ID and due diligence documents from new clients, chases what's missing and puts the file in front of your compliance person.
  • Chasing clients to approve their tax return: Sends each finished return for approval with a short summary, reminds the client until they approve or ask a question, and saves the signed approval before you file.

Trades & contractors

  • CIS subcontractor invoices before the monthly return: Collects every subcontractor's invoice and checks verification details after the tax month ends, so the CIS return is ready before the 19th.
  • Following up estimates and quotes: Every open estimate gets polite follow-ups on a fixed schedule until the customer says yes or no.
  • WhatsApp quote follow-up: Three short WhatsApp messages after each quote, then it stops, with every reply flagged to you.
  • Texting back missed calls: Anyone whose call you miss gets a text within a minute, a few questions about the job and a way to book.
  • Tracking subcontractor insurance certificates: Collects a current COI from every sub, logs expiry dates and asks for the renewal before it lapses.
  • Change orders signed before the work: The crew logs a change on a phone, the office prices it and it goes for e-signature before any extra work starts.
  • Maintenance agreement renewals: Keeps one list of every plan, reminds customers before it lapses and asks them to book their covered tune-ups.
  • EICR remedial work follow-up: Sends the remedial quote within a day of an unsatisfactory EICR and follows up on dates so the work is approved in time.
  • Out-of-hours emergency call triage: Every out-of-hours caller answers the same short questions, so only real emergencies wake the engineer on call.
  • Job sign-off and the final invoice: Completion photos and the customer's signature on a phone, then a draft final invoice the same day.
  • Solar and heat pump job paperwork, enquiry to handover: Tracks each solar or heat pump job from enquiry to handover, chases quotes and customer documents, and builds the handover pack with certificate and warranty dates in view.
  • Decorator quotes and colour sign-off: Sends each decorating quote the same day and follows it up, then collects a written sign-off of every colour and finish before paint is ordered, and keeps the customer informed through the job.
  • Roofing inspection reports and quote follow-up: Turns the photos and notes from the roof into a report and quote sent the same day, follows the quote up until the customer answers, and keeps them informed once the job is booked.
  • RFI and submittal tracking for a general contractor: Keeps one numbered log of RFIs and submittals per job, sends each one, reminds architects and subs before the due date from your contract, files the answers and lists what is late.
  • Daily site reports from the phone to the office: Site leads fill in one phone form each day; the report is filed in the job folder, sent to the office, flags changes and incidents at once, and becomes a weekly client update.

Letting agents & property managers

  • Tenant repair requests: Every repair report comes in on one form with photos, urgent jobs go to a person and the tenant is updated at each step.
  • Getting landlords to approve repairs: Checks each job against the landlord's limit, sends the quote for approval, chases on a schedule and logs the decision.
  • Gas safety, EICR and EPC reminders: One list of every property and certificate, with renewals started weeks before each expiry date.
  • Right to rent follow-up checks: Tracks every time-limited right to rent and asks the tenant for evidence well before the follow-up date.
  • Deposit protection 30-day clock: Starts a 30-day clock when a deposit arrives and chases protection and the prescribed information until a person confirms both.
  • Rent review and lease renewal reminders: Tracks each tenancy's review date, asks the landlord for a decision and drafts the notice for a person to check.
  • Property inspection scheduling: Books each inspection, gives the tenant written notice, reminds everyone and sends the report to the landlord.
  • Sales progression from offer to completion: Chases both solicitors and the buyer's mortgage milestone by milestone, updates buyer and seller, and flags sales that have gone quiet.
  • Survey bookings, access and report delivery: Takes each survey instruction, gets the terms signed, books access with the agent, collects the buyer's questions and sends the report with an offer to discuss it.
  • Following up real estate leads and booking showings: Replies to every portal, website and missed-call lead within minutes, books showings, reminds you who to call and keeps in touch with buyers who aren't ready yet.
  • Service charge demands and reminders for block managers: Sends service charge demands with the summary of rights attached, reminds leaseholders who haven't paid, pauses chasing while a query is open and lists unpaid flats for you.
  • Collecting rental application documents: Sends every rental applicant the same document checklist, files each upload, reminds them about anything missing, chases references and tells you when the file is ready to review.
  • Rent arrears reminders and landlord updates: Spots missed rent the day after it's due, sends firm and fair reminders, keeps a full record, tracks repayment plans and keeps the landlord updated, while a person decides every legal step.
  • Guest messages and cleaner schedule for short-term rentals: Sends each guest the booking, check-in, checkout and review messages on time, and keeps the cleaner's turnover schedule in step with every booking and cancellation.

Solicitors & conveyancers

  • New client intake for solicitors: An instant reply with a short form, a conflict check before any booking, the client care letter and ID requests.
  • Conveyancing client updates: A short message to the client each time their file reaches a milestone, so fewer "any update?" calls.
  • Source of funds document chasing: Sends the right document list for each way of funding, chases what's missing and shows which files are ready for review.
  • Will questionnaire before the meeting: Sends the questionnaire the day a client gets in touch, reminds until it's complete, then offers the meeting.
  • Immigration case document checklists: Sends each client the document checklist for their case type, records what arrives, reminds them about what's missing and warns the fee earner before a deadline.
  • Collecting probate documents from executors: Sends executors one checklist of the papers and facts a probate file needs, files what comes back, chases only what's missing and tracks letters to banks.

Mortgage & insurance brokers

  • Mortgage document collection: Sends each client the checklist for their situation, chases only what's missing and shows whose pack is ready.
  • Insurance renewal reminders: Every renewal date triggers a details request, reminders and a renewal email prepared for a broker to check.
  • Keeping clients updated on insurance claims: Keeps one claims log, requests the documents each claim needs, sends clients status updates on a schedule and drafts chasers to insurers when replies are overdue.

Clinics & practices (physio, dental, vets, opticians)

  • Patient intake forms before the first visit: Sends the right intake form when a new patient books and reminds until it's back.
  • Rebooking patients mid-treatment: Finds patients with sessions left and no next booking, offers a booking link and hands the rest to a person.
  • Dental recall follow-up: Follows up patients who ignore the first recall reminder and gives the front desk one call list.
  • Appointment reminders and no-shows: Confirms every booking, reminds the day before with an easy way to confirm or move, and lists who didn't reply.
  • Vaccination, booster and flea and worm reminders: Follows up owners who ignore the first booster reminder, groups pets in one household, and gives reception one weekly call list.
  • Eye test recall and contact lens aftercare reminders: Follows up patients who ignore the first eye test recall, runs a separate aftercare track for contact lens wearers and gives the team one call list.
  • Recall reminders for a hearing care practice: Reminds each patient when their hearing check, hearing aid service or follow-up is due, confirms bookings, flags warranty end dates and lists who hasn't rebooked for a personal call.
  • Consent forms and aftercare messages for a med spa: Sends the right medical history and consent form when a client books, chases it before the appointment, sends your written aftercare after treatment and invites clients back for a review or top-up.
  • Client intake and session reminders for a therapy practice: Sends new clients the intake, consent and cancellation policy forms, chases the ones not back, files them to the record and sends discreet session reminders.

Financial advisers & planners

  • Annual review paperwork for ongoing advice clients: Invites each ongoing client when their review is due, chases the fact-find and any ID refresh, logs every attempt to engage and gets the suitability report signed.

Architects, photographers & event planners

  • Fee proposal follow-up and stage approvals for architects: Follows up every fee proposal until the client answers, sends the terms for signature, and gets a dated written approval at each design stage in one sign-off log.
  • Bookings, contracts and deposits: Takes each shoot from enquiry to gallery: availability reply, contract and deposit, questionnaire, balance reminder, delivery and a review request.
  • Payment schedules and supplier deadlines for weddings and events: Builds each booking's schedule back from the event date: the couple's instalment reminders, supplier deposits and confirmations, the final details questionnaire and one shared timeline.
  • Artwork approvals for a sign and print shop: Collects artwork against your spec, sends each proof with an approve or request-changes choice, chases until answered, records the approved version before production and keeps the customer informed to collection or install.
  • Wedding and event orders for a florist: Replies to wedding enquiries, books consultations, sends proposals and deposit requests, then collects final counts and venue details on a timeline and builds the week-before checklist.
  • Client approvals, orders and deliveries for an interior designer: Sends each selection to the client for written approval, waits for payment, drafts purchase orders for you to release and keeps one tracker of orders and deliveries.

Tutors & instructors

  • Enrolment, termly invoices and parent updates: Takes each family from enquiry to trial lesson and enrolment, drafts each term's invoices from the register, reminds unpaid parents and sends approved attendance and progress updates.
  • Lesson reminders, block payments and test dates: Reminds pupils before every lesson, sends a payment link when a block is nearly used, and keeps test and theory dates so you talk about readiness in time.
  • Lesson scheduling and term fees for a music school: Runs the timetable, lesson reminders, make-up slots and teacher-absence messages from one list, builds each term's invoices from the timetable and chases unpaid fees and exam entries.
  • Term bookings and waiting list for a swim school: Runs re-enrolment before each term ends, sends fee requests and reminders, offers freed places to matching families on the waiting list and confirms stage moves to parents.
  • Nursery waiting list and enrolment paperwork: Keeps one waiting list with each family's start date, days and funding, sends place offers and the enrolment pack, chases missing form details and funding codes, and reminds parents to reconfirm their code.

Home care agencies

  • Carer compliance documents and rota change messages: Chases each carer's missing documents, reminds before training, licences or right to work run out, and sends rota changes to carers with a reply to confirm.

Home services (removals, pest control, pet care)

  • Removals surveys, bookings and move-day messages: Books the home or video survey, follows up the quote, takes the deposit, sends the pre-move checklist and move-day confirmations, then asks for a review.
  • Pest control contract visits, reports and renewals: Schedules every routine contract visit, tells the site contact, turns the technician's form into a visit report, books follow-ups and reminds before renewal.
  • Dog bookings, rebooking and vaccination records: Sends booking reminders and rebooking links, collects intake and consent forms, and asks for updated vaccination records before they expire.
  • Pool service visit reports, repair approvals and billing: Sends the pool owner a report with readings and photos after every visit, gets repairs approved in writing and reminds owners about the service bill.

IT support & MSPs

  • New client onboarding and renewal reminders: Collects access, assets and contacts from each new client, then reminds you about contract, licence, domain and certificate renewals and books quarterly reviews.

Charities & nonprofits

  • Gift Aid declarations and claims: Every Gift Aid declaration is captured with the donation, stored as evidence, confirmed to the donor, and each claim list is ready for your treasurer to check.
  • Grant reporting deadlines and report drafts: Keeps every grant report date in one calendar, reminds the right people in time, collects figures and stories from staff and drafts each report for a person to approve.

Garages & motor trade

  • MOT and service reminders for a garage: Reminds each customer before their MOT or service is due, confirms bookings, gets extra work approved in writing and texts when the car is ready.
  • Service bookings and repair updates for a bike shop: Confirms service bookings, sends drop-off receipts, texts the mechanic's quote for approval, reports parts delays, sends the ready-to-collect message and reminds customers when the next service is due.
  • Repair status updates and insurer chasing for a body shop: Texts the customer at each repair stage, gets extra work approved in writing and chases insurers for authorisations and supplements on a schedule.

Salons, gyms & studios

  • Cancellations, deposits and empty slots: Shows your policy at booking, fills cancelled slots from a waiting list and keeps a log of deposits kept or refunded.
  • Failed membership payments: Picks up each failed payment, sends a friendly link to update details and puts members still unpaid on a call list.
  • Memberships and gradings for a martial arts school: Follows up trial students, sends membership paperwork and payment set-up, chases failed payments, counts attendance against grading requirements and sends grading invitations once the instructor approves.
  • Weekly check-ins and package renewals for a personal trainer: Confirms and reminds each session, sends a weekly check-in form, chases clients who haven't answered, files progress photos only with consent, flags answers about pain, and reminds clients before their package runs out.
  • Deposits, consent forms and aftercare for a tattoo studio: Takes the deposit with your policy, sends the consent and health form before the session, flags answers for the artist, and sends aftercare and touch-up messages.
  • Intro offer and class-pack follow-up for a fitness studio: Welcomes each new intro-offer client, checks in after their first class, nudges them if they stop booking, sets out the membership options before the offer ends, and reminds pack holders before credits expire.

Cleaning & garden services

  • Recurring cleaning admin: Builds the week's rota, reminds clients, sends each cleaner a job sheet and invoices after each visit.
  • Seasonal renewals: Asks last season's customers to sign up again with a price and link, reminds the undecided and books the first round.
  • Repeat booking reminders for a carpet cleaner: Reminds past customers when their next clean is likely due, follows up quotes, confirms bookings with an on-the-way text, sends aftercare and asks once for a review, with opt-outs recorded.

Marketing agencies

  • New client onboarding: From an accepted proposal to a ready kickoff: contract, first invoice, brief form, files and access requests.
  • Monthly client reports: Pulls the month's figures into each client's report on a fixed day, so a person only writes the commentary.
  • Automations for your clients, under your brand: A workflow your agency sells to its own client, built in the client's accounts behind your brand, with you as their point of contact.

Recruitment agencies

  • Weekly timesheet chasing: Reminds workers when the week ends, asks the client's approver once hours arrive and lists gaps on payroll morning.
  • Candidate compliance documents: One checklist per role, an automatic request when a candidate registers, reminders and an expiry list.

Restaurants, venues & caterers

  • Custom cake orders and deposits: Replies to cake enquiries with your lead time and prices, collects the design, date, servings and allergen answers on one form, takes the deposit and balance on schedule and sends the collection message.
  • Venue hire enquiries from first reply to final payment: Replies to each venue enquiry fast, books viewings, sends proposals, reminds before a date hold ends, sends the contract and chases each payment and the final details.
  • Catering quote follow-up and final numbers: Follows up catering quotes until the client books or says no, then collects final guest numbers and dietary requirements by your cut-off and builds the kitchen's event sheet.
  • Private dining and group booking enquiries: Replies to every group or private dining enquiry within minutes, holds the date, chases the deposit, collects final numbers and dietary needs, and sends the kitchen one sheet.

Another service business

  • Invoice payment reminders: Polite reminders before and after the due date that stop the moment a client pays.
  • Supplier invoice approvals: Sends each supplier invoice to the right approver, reminds them until they answer and records who approved what.
  • Replying to new enquiries fast: Every enquiry lands in one list and gets an honest first reply straight away, with the questions you need answered.
  • Asking every customer for a review: One short request with your review link after each job, and one reminder.
  • AI that sorts enquiries and drafts replies: An AI agent sorts incoming messages and drafts replies; a person approves before anything reaches a customer.
  • Coaching and consulting client onboarding: From discovery call to first session: proposal, contract, invoice, intake questionnaire, session booking and a package tracker with renewal reminders.
  • Translation quote requests and file intake: Every translation request comes in through one form, files go to a job folder, a draft quote is ready for approval, and accepted jobs are assigned, delivered and invoiced.
  • Job updates for a phone repair shop: Sends the check-in receipt, the diagnosis and price to approve by text, parts-delay messages, the ready-to-collect text and one review request, from each status change on the job.
  • Proof of delivery and failed delivery messages for a courier: Sends the proof of delivery to the customer as soon as a drop is done, rebooks failed deliveries by text and attaches each POD to the invoice draft.
  • SIA licence tracking and shift confirmations: Keeps every officer's SIA licence and first aid expiry dates in one list, reminds them to renew in time, and confirms each shift by text after checking the licence is valid.
